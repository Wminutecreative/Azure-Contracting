The project consisted of a full Office fit-out and complete M&E and AV installation over 3 floors. The works were focused around the following areas.

Ground Floor – Construction of five enclosed, acoustic glazed screened meetings rooms, a large fully equipped staff canteen, multipurpose room, Atrium/ reception area, shower/changing room, all of which received bespoke joinery.
